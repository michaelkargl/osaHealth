**Reports to:** Michael Kargl (Founder)

**Process owner:** Miriam Kovač, Head of People & Talent

---

## What We Build

osaHealth is a health-data product in the sleep and respiratory domain. The work is technically sophisticated — distributed systems, offline-first mobile architecture, sensitive clinical data — and the team is small, senior, and deliberate about what it ships.

We are not a sprint factory. We are a team that cares about building the right things well, and we need a PM who understands that distinction.

---

## The Role

You will own delivery at osaHealth. That means the rhythm, the clarity, the communication, and the accountability. Not through process for its own sake — through the judgment to know when structure helps and when it gets in the way.

You report to Michael. You work alongside Vivica, who leads the team day-to-day. You are not above anyone on this team; you are the person who makes it possible for everyone else to do their best work without losing track of where things are going.

---

## What You'll Own

**The delivery cadence.** Standups aren't the cadence — you are. When something is slipping, you catch it before it becomes a problem. When a decision is stalled, you surface it and create the conditions for it to get made.

**Communication between the team and stakeholders.** You translate in both directions — without distortion. The founder and external partners get accurate, calm updates. The team gets clear direction and protected focus time.

**Risk visibility.** There is no formal risk register here yet. There will be one, because you'll build it. Not because someone told you to, but because you understand what happens to a small team when untracked risk becomes a crisis.

**Process design.** We have process. Some of it is working; some of it is legacy habit. You'll identify the difference and make the case for change when the evidence supports it.

**The seam between engineering and product.** You work directly with the architect and engineers. You don't need to understand distributed systems — you need to understand what questions to ask, and you need engineers to want you in the room when hard scoping decisions are being made.

---

## What Success Looks Like

**At 30 days:** You have a coherent picture of current state. You have identified at least one thing that is silently broken and named it with evidence.

**At 60 days:** You are running the delivery cadence independently. The team is no longer scheduling coordination meetings — things are happening because you made them happen. Vivica is no longer carrying the coordination overhead.

**At 90 days:** You have shipped at least one process change that made the team visibly faster. Stakeholders have noticed the improvement in communication quality. Michael is being informed rather than managing.

---

## What We Need From You

**Must-have:**

- A track record of running delivery in a software team — shipped products, real timelines, real consequences when something slipped
- Async-first communication as a default, not a workaround — your written updates are clear, actionable, and appropriately short
- The ability to talk to engineers about technical tradeoffs without needing translation — not to be technical, but to be trusted by people who are
- Stakeholder management under genuine ambiguity — you've told someone something they didn't want to hear and the relationship was stronger for it
- Comfort in a small team where process is still forming — you have opinions and the patience to earn buy-in

**Nice to have:**

- Experience in health, medical devices, or other regulated domains where data sensitivity isn't abstract
- Background in a founder-led or early-stage organisation — you know what "wearing many hats" actually means in practice
- Familiarity with async delivery tooling (Linear, Notion, GitHub) — not the tool, the discipline
- Experience with BLE/sensor or offline-first products — relevant, not required

---

## What You Won't Find Here

This is not a role for someone who wants fully-defined processes and a team under them waiting for direction. The process is partly yours to build. The team is senior and opinionated. Your authority comes from your judgment, not your title.

If you need a playbook to feel productive, this isn't the right fit. If ambiguity is where you do your best work — this might be exactly right.

## Our Process

Five rounds. Structured. Predictable. We publish the comp band because we're not interested in playing games about it. You'll know what the full process looks like at Round 1. No surprises, no artificial urgency.

**Round 1** — 45 min structured call with Miriam (Screening)  
**Round 2** — 75 min delivery scenario with Vivica  
**Round 3** — 60 min values conversation with Michael  
**Round 4** — Async writing sample (48 hours, no meeting)  
**Round 5** — 45 min technical collaboration conversation with Remy  

We respond to every application within 3 business days.
