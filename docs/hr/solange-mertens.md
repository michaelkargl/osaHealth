### 1. Disagreement with authority

> *"Tell me about a time you disagreed with someone who had more power than you in the situation. How did you handle it?"*

**Your answer:** This was in clinical supervision, about fifteen years into my practice. I said I disagreed, that I was reading her attachment as healthy rather than defensive, and that I thought pursuing the resistance framing would do real clinical harm. He was not accustomed to being challenged in case review. I didn't soften the disagreement, but I framed it as a clinical question — I told him I needed him to help me understand what specifically he was observing that I wasn't, because we were reaching incompatible formulations of the same presentation. The conversation that followed was the most educationally useful supervision I had that year, even though his conclusion didn't change. Mine didn't either. I changed supervisors within the year — not because of that conversation, but because it made visible a fundamental framework mismatch I'd been aware of for some time.
