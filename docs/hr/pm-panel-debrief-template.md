## Instructions

Each interviewer completes their section **independently** before the debrief call. Do not discuss the candidate with other panel members before submitting your scorecard. Scores and notes are submitted to Miriam no later than 2 hours before the scheduled debrief.

Scoring scale: **1–4 only. No half-scores. No abstentions.**

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **4** | Would specifically want this person to represent us in a hard situation |
| **3** | Solid, no concerns, would work fine |
| **2** | Would work with caveats — specific concern must be named |
| **1** | Hard no, or requires substantially more evidence |

**Hire bar:** Average ≥ 3.2 across all six dimensions; no dimension at 1; unanimous panel agreement.  
**Best-of-best bar:** Average ≥ 3.5; at least two dimensions at 4; no dimension below 2.

---

## Scoring Dimensions

| Dimension | What You're Evaluating |
|---|---|
| **Delivery clarity** | Articulates what "done" means; catches slippage before it's a crisis |
| **Stakeholder handling** | Manages up and sideways without drama; leaves people better informed |
| **Async communication** | Written quality: clear, actionable, appropriate length |
| **Ambiguity tolerance** | Energised by undefined problems; creates clarity where others see chaos |
| **Technical vocabulary** | Holds their own with engineers; asks good questions; doesn't pretend |
| **Self-awareness** | Accurate model of their own strengths and gaps; specific about where they need support |
